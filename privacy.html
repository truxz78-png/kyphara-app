Privacy Policy for Kyphara

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This page was translated automatically. The English version is the official one.

Kyphara ("the App") is a free guide to beauty stores, package forwarding services and online beauty courses, published by DriftforgeDev, an independent developer from the Dominican Republic.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses and why.

1. No account and no personal information

The App does not ask you to create an account. The App does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The App does not sell personal information. The App does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

2. What the App saves on your phone

  • The language you choose in the App.
  • Translations of the App's text, so the App loads faster next time.
  • A copy of the App's own pages, so the App can open without an internet connection.

This information stays on your phone. It is not sent to the developer. You can delete it by clearing the App's data in your phone's settings.

3. Services the App uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the App's pages. Like any web server, GitHub may receive technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.
  • Google Fonts provides the App's fonts. Google receives technical data, such as your IP address, when the fonts load.
  • Google Translate translates the App's text when your language is not English. Only the App's own text is sent. Your personal information is never sent.

Each of these companies handles that technical data under its own privacy policy.

4. Partner stores and affiliate links

When you tap a store button, the store's website opens outside the App. The store and the affiliate network that records the visit may use cookies and collect information under their own privacy policies.

If you buy something, the store handles your order, payment and delivery data. The App does not receive that data. The developer may earn a commission on purchases made through these links, at no extra cost to you.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children. The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
