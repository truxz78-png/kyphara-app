A simple daily routine
Morning:
gentle cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher).
Night:
remove makeup, cleanse, then apply moisturizer.
Once or twice a week:
a gentle exfoliant or a face mask.
Try one new product at a time. Before using it on your face, test a small amount behind your ear or on your inner arm for 24 hours.
Find your undertone
Cool:
the veins on your wrist look blue or purple, and silver jewelry suits you best.
Warm:
your veins look green, and gold jewelry suits you best.
Neutral:
you see both colors, and both silver and gold suit you.
Choose foundation and concealer with the same undertone as your skin, and test the color on your jawline in daylight.
Makeup brushes: the basics
Large fluffy brush:
powder and bronzer.
Flat dense brush:
liquid foundation.
Small round brush:
eyeshadow.
Angled brush:
blush, contour and eyebrows.
Wash your brushes every week with mild soap and let them dry lying flat.
Healthy nails
File in one direction only, not back and forth.
Moisturize your cuticles with oil every night.
Always use a base coat before colored polish.
Give your nails a few days off between gel manicures.
Beard care
Wash your beard two or three times a week with a beard shampoo.
Apply a few drops of beard oil on damp hair to soften it.
Comb in the direction of growth and trim the neckline every week.

Dry skin
Use a creamy cleanser and a rich moisturizer. Avoid very hot water.
Oily skin
Use a gel cleanser and a light, oil-free moisturizer. Do not skip moisturizer.
Combination skin
Use a gentle cleanser, a light moisturizer, and blotting papers for shiny areas.
Normal skin
Keep it simple: gentle cleanser, moisturizer and daily sunscreen.
Sensitive skin
Choose fragrance-free products and test each new product before using it on your face.
