Beauty guides

Skin type, daily routine, undertone, nails, beard and perfume

What is your skin type?

A simple daily routine

  1. Morning: gentle cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher).
  2. Night: remove makeup, cleanse, then apply moisturizer.
  3. Once or twice a week: a gentle exfoliant or a face mask.

Try one new product at a time. Before using it on your face, test a small amount behind your ear or on your inner arm for 24 hours.

Find your undertone

  • Cool: the veins on your wrist look blue or purple, and silver jewelry suits you best.
  • Warm: your veins look green, and gold jewelry suits you best.
  • Neutral: you see both colors, and both silver and gold suit you.

Choose foundation and concealer with the same undertone as your skin, and test the color on your jawline in daylight.

Makeup brushes: the basics

  • Large fluffy brush: powder and bronzer.
  • Flat dense brush: liquid foundation.
  • Small round brush: eyeshadow.
  • Angled brush: blush, contour and eyebrows.

Wash your brushes every week with mild soap and let them dry lying flat.

Healthy nails

  • File in one direction only, not back and forth.
  • Moisturize your cuticles with oil every night.
  • Always use a base coat before colored polish.
  • Give your nails a few days off between gel manicures.

Beard care

  • Wash your beard two or three times a week with a beard shampoo.
  • Apply a few drops of beard oil on damp hair to soften it.
  • Comb in the direction of growth and trim the neckline every week.

Understanding perfume

TypeFragrance oilLasts about
Eau de Cologne2–4%2 hours
Eau de Toilette5–15%3 to 5 hours
Eau de Parfum15–20%5 to 8 hours
Parfum20–30%8 hours or more

Spray on pulse points: wrists, neck and behind the ears. Do not rub your wrists together, because it breaks the fragrance faster.

Buy perfume from official stores: very cheap copies of famous brands are often fake.
